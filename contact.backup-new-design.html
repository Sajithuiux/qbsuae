FAQs

Common questions about business setup.

How long does company formation take? +

Freezone companies can be incorporated in as little as 3–5 business days. Mainland DED licenses typically take 5–10 business days. Offshore structures can be completed within 48–72 hours.

Do I need to be physically present in Dubai? +

In most cases, no. We handle the full process remotely with notarized documents. For certain mainland activities or bank account openings, a brief visit may be required.

What's the difference between Mainland, Freezone, and Offshore? +

Mainland allows direct UAE market trading. Freezone offers 100% foreign ownership with tax benefits. Offshore is for holding structures and international operations with no UAE residency or local trading rights.

What documents do I need to get started? +

Typically a valid passport copy, visa page, and Emirates ID (if UAE-based), plus business activity details and a proposed trade name. We'll provide a full checklist for your setup type.

Can QBS help with opening a corporate bank account? +

Yes. We assist clients in opening accounts with leading UAE banks as part of our post-formation support, including document preparation and bank relationship introductions.
