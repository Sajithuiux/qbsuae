What Is a Dubai Commercial License?

A commercial license in Dubai authorizes a company to buy and sell goods and services. Businesses involved in trading activities—such as electronics, construction materials, or general merchandise—typically operate under a commercial license. However, companies that manufacture products must instead obtain an industrial license. A commercial license can cover a wide range of business activities, including:

General trading
Contracting
Import and export
Supply and brokerage
Vehicle rental
Logistics
Healthcare product trading

A Dubai commercial license allows a company to operate up to 10 business activities under one license.

Trade License vs. Commercial License

In Dubai, the term "trade license" is commonly used as a general term for business licenses. A commercial license is therefore a type of trade license specifically used for trading-related activities.

Legal Structure Allowed

Dubai does not restrict the legal form associated with a commercial license. Both sole establishments and limited liability companies (LLCs) can apply for one, as long as their activities fall within the trading category.

Steps to Register and Obtain a Commercial Trade License in Dubai

1
Select your business activities that fall under commercial trading.
2
Prepare the company's Memorandum of Association (MOA).
3
Register with Dubai's Department of Economic Development (DED) to obtain a VAT number.
4
Pay the required government fees for company registration and license issuance.

Commercial License Validity & Renewal

Validity Period

A commercial license is valid for one year from the date of issuance. Upon renewal, the license becomes valid for four years.

Required for Renewal
  • The original commercial license (or a copy)
  • A valid office lease contract (Ejari)
  • Required approvals from relevant authorities
  • Employee accommodation details (if foreign shareholders are involved)

Types of Tourism Licenses

Inbound Tourism License

Lets a company organise and manage trips for visitors entering the UAE — including leisure, corporate, conference, exhibition, and business travel. Ideal for tour operators welcoming international visitors to Dubai.

Outbound Tourism License

Covers companies that assist UAE residents travelling abroad. These businesses may operate inside or outside the UAE and can help with international holiday packages, group tours, and corporate travel.

Travel Agent / Agency License

Lets an agency or a single licensed agent arrange international flight bookings and assist with travel visas. Suitable for travel agencies dealing with both inbound and outbound travel arrangements.

Required Documents

  • Completed application form
  • Passport and visa copies of all partners and shareholders
  • Attested educational qualifications and professional accreditation certificates
  • NOC from the Civil Aviation Authority (where applicable)
  • Attested proof of the company's office location

Visa Types We Handle

Employment Visa

Required for foreign nationals working in the UAE. Follows an Entry Permit and Work Permit. Free Zone workers also need a Free Zone visa, and a mandatory medical exam is part of the process. QBS coordinates the full sequence.

Resident Visa

For foreigners relocating to the UAE. Valid for three years and renewable. A resident visa is required for banking, obtaining a driving licence, vehicle registration, and applying for a Dubai Business License.

Additional Visa Services

Steps to Obtain a Trade License

Determine the correct activity category for your business.
2
Prepare the appropriate legal structure (LLC, sole proprietorship, branch, etc.).
3
Obtain initial approvals from relevant government authorities.
4
Choose and register a unique trade name with the DED.

Lease suitable premises and obtain the required Ejari approval.
6
Prepare the Memorandum of Association (MOA) and submit all documents to the DED.
Annual Renewal

Trade licenses must be renewed every year. Start the renewal process at least one month before expiry. Government fees are generally AED 8,000–15,000 depending on business activity and license type.

Renewal Checklist
  • Obtain any special activity approvals
  • Confirm all permits and NOCs are valid
  • Settle any outstanding DED fines
  • Renew office lease via Ejari
  • Submit renewal application and pay the fee
